# Severity model

| SEV | Impact | Response |
|-----|--------|----------|
| SEV-1 | Critical customer / revenue outage | Immediate all-hands IR |
| SEV-2 | Major degradation or partial outage | Urgent, dedicated IC |
| SEV-3 | Limited impact, workaround exists | Business hours ok |
| SEV-4 | Minor / cosmetic | Backlog |
